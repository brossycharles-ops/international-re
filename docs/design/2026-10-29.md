## Improvements shipped
1. **Retirement-visa table: built to be cited and readable on phones.** File: guides/retirement-visa-income-requirements-latin-america-2026.html. This was the Growth Director's brief.
   - On phones it is now a real table that scrolls sideways, with the Country column pinned. Before, it was 10 stacked cards about 5,000px long.
   - "Minimum monthly pension or income" moved to the 2nd column (markup order only, no text changed), so Country + amount show without swiping.
   - The scroll region can be reached with the keyboard and shows a visible focus ring.
   - A "Cite this table" box sits under the table, with a ready-made citation (title + URL) and a Copy citation button. The button uses the existing `data-action="copy-link"` in lead_capture.js.
   - The wide desktop table is unchanged.
2. **Starter Kit callout became a real signup.** Files: public/lead_capture.js, public/layout_updates.css, plus 6 articles.
   - Every `p.kit-callout` (23 pages, and every future Growth Engine article that uses it) is wrapped in a card with an email field and a "Send me the free kit" button.
   - Signups are tagged `kit-callout`. It is hidden once the reader has subscribed.
   - Before, it was grey text with an unstyled link to the Newsletter Archive.
   - Tested on localhost: the signup is stored with source `kit-callout` and the reader is redirected to /thankyou.html.
   - Brief item 2: a topic-matched callout was added after the first answer on the 6 most-read articles that had none: blog/best-places-retire-latin-america-2026.html, blog/best-places-retire-mexico-2026.html, blog/medellin-real-estate-2026.html, blog/argentina-real-estate-foreigners-2026.html, blog/how-to-buy-property-costa-rica-foreigner.html and guides/cuenca-ecuador-neighborhood-guide-property-buyers-2026.html. The other top-5 pages, Cuenca cost of living and Tbilisi, already had one. Home has its hero form; contact is not an article.
   - Shared: `.blog-table-wrap` now scrolls sideways (it had no CSS before).
3. **Outliers onto the standard template (Mexico City, Argentina)** — details above. retire-in-argentina is in the retirement cluster Growth is pushing.
4. **Phone menu fixed on every article (found this week).** File: public/layout_updates.css.
   - Live, on any page whose navbar is `.scrolled` (every article, and home after scrolling), the menu opened as a 68px strip of see-through links over the page.
   - Cause: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` on `.navbar.scrolled` (styles.css:89) traps the fixed full-screen menu inside the bar.
   - Fix: at ≤768px the blur is turned off (the bar is 96% opaque anyway) and the open menu gets a solid navy background. The menu is now 844px tall on all 5 pages tested.
5. **Image weight on desktop.** Files: 128 pages.
   - Card images on home, blog.html and guides.html declared `sizes="…, 1200px"`, so browsers fetched 1024–1600px photos for 270–630px cards. They now declare 300/560/640px.
   - Every srcset gained a `w=1280&q=70 1280w` candidate (215 srcsets), so 1280–1440px desktop screens stop downloading the 1600px hero.
   - All 36 new image URLs return 200. Same photos, same alt text.

Cache busting: layout_updates.css and lead_capture.js are now `?v=20261029` on every page, in templates/blog-post.html and in scripts/generate_city_hubs.py. The city hubs were regenerated, and rebuild_sitemap.py restored the breadcrumbs.
