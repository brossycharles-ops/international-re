## Improvements shipped
1. **Standard header and footer on the 11 outliers** (public/blog/*, listed above). These are mostly the retirement cluster that Growth is pushing, including the #4 most-read page. Phones get a working menu, desktop gets the full nav and Starter Kit button, and the footer has the standard columns plus a Privacy link. Their 1400px hero photos got srcset (phones load 640px), and related-card thumbnails now lazy-load. Article body text is unchanged.
2. **Signup UI: one ask at a time, readable, lighter** (public/layout_updates.css, public/lead_capture.js, public/styles.css, public/index.html):
   - The exit-intent modal now sits above the sticky subscribe bar (z-index 9500 vs 9000). Before, both were visible at once on phones.
   - The modal's fine print was squeezed into half the grid width; it now spans the full width.
   - On phones (≤600px) the modal drops its photo so the form shows without scrolling. The lazy image is then never fetched, saving 210 KB per article view. On desktop the photo is w=800&q=70 instead of w=1200, with alt text from data/images.json.
   - Sticky-bar input and buttons have a visible gold focus ring (it was `outline:none`).
   - Author-box bio colour #a1a1aa → #52525b on #f4f4f5 (about 2.3:1 → about 7:1, WCAG AA) on 67 articles.
   - The home hero preload now uses imagesrcset, so phones stop downloading the 1920px hero on top of the 640px one.
   - The hero `<img>` on 16 more pages got srcset, including the Starter Kit page and 10 country/cost-of-living articles.
3. **City hubs: spacing and image weight** (public/cities.css, scripts/generate_city_hubs.py, regenerated public/cities/, breadcrumbs restored via rebuild_sitemap.py):
   - layout_updates.css's global `section{padding-block:96px}` was adding about 250px gaps between hub sections; `.hub-section{padding-block:0}` fixes it.
   - Hero background: w=800&q=70 on phones, w=1600&q=75 on desktop (was w=1920 everywhere).
   - City-index cards: w=600&q=70 (was w=800&q=80).

Cache busting: styles.css, layout_updates.css, lead_capture.js and cities.css are now `?v=20261022` on every page, the generator and templates/blog-post.html. The template previously linked styles.css, layout_updates.css and lead_capture.js with no version, so new articles could pick up stale CSS.

Checks: visual_audit.js against localhost found 0 findings in 30 views. verify_site.sh printed PASS (153 URLs crawled). The mobile menu was clicked open on 5 pages.

## Backlog (ranked)
1. blog.html and guides.html: card images are w=1600 with no srcset, 9–11 MB on desktop after scrolling. Use w=600 cards with srcset and lazy-load below the fold.
2. templates/blog-post.html hero `<img>` has no srcset. The `{{HERO_IMAGE}}` placeholder is a full URL, so the Growth Engine (or a post-step) needs to emit srcset/sizes.
3. Mobile exit-intent fires on any 24px scroll-up after 600px (lead_capture.js initExitIntent), which is aggressive on phones where the sticky bar is already showing. Consider phones only at 70% depth, or not at all.
4. Bring the two `top-nav` pages (cost-of-living-mexico-city, retire-in-argentina) onto styles.css with the standard nav. This needs care because their inline typography differs.
5. Unify article footers (about 12 variants) and add Cities to the standard nav (template + all pages) so the 17 city hubs are reachable from articles.
6. City hubs: switch the white `.hub-nav` to the standard dark navbar with a Starter Kit button, via the generator.
7. Home on phones: the hero H1 sits tight under the navbar, and the "Browse All Issues" and "Subscribe Free" buttons in the newsletter block touch and are misaligned.
8. Desktop Tulum hub hero is still 836 KB at w=1600&q=75. Try w=1400&q=65 or an image-set with webp.
