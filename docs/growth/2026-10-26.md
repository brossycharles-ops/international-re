1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters and sign in with a Microsoft, Google or Facebook account.
2. Choose **Import your sites from Google Search Console**, then **Continue**, and allow access with the Google account that owns the internationalre.org Search Console property.
3. Tick www.internationalre.org (or the domain property), then **Import**. Verification carries over and the sitemap is imported.
4. Open **Sitemaps** and confirm https://www.internationalre.org/sitemap.xml shows. If it doesn't, **Submit sitemap** with that URL.
5. Optional: under **Settings → IndexNow**, confirm submissions from the key `internationalre` appear. The agents already ping IndexNow on every publish.
6. Next Monday, export **Search Performance** (last 7 days, queries and pages) next to the Search Console export.

**2. Pinterest auto-pins from the RSS feed (about 15 minutes; one setup, then every new article becomes a pin)**
1. At https://business.pinterest.com, create a free business account, or convert your personal account under Settings → Account management → Convert to business.
2. Claim the website: **Settings → Claimed accounts → Websites → Claim**, choose the **HTML tag** option, copy the `<meta name="p:domain_verify" ...>` tag, and add it to docs/IDEAS.md or paste it into the next session. The agents will add it to public/index.html (no password needed).
3. Create 3 boards: "Retire in Latin America", "Buying Property in Latin America", "Cost of Living Latin America".
4. Go to **Settings → Bulk create Pins → Auto-publish → Connect RSS feed**. Enter https://www.internationalre.org/feed.xml and pick the "Buying Property in Latin America" board. You can add the same feed again for another board.
5. Pins appear within about 24 hours of each new article. The 6 Pinterest texts in today's kit can be pinned by hand to the matching boards in the meantime.
