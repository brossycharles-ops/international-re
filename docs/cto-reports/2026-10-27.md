## Status (HEALTHY / ISSUES / P0 ALERT)
ISSUES. Two P0 content problems were found and fixed today, both on pages visitors read for money decisions. The site is up, all 156 sitemap URLs return 200, and every form delivers.

## Alerts
- **P0 (fixed): false "no capital gains tax" claim.** `/quick-reads/property-tax-uruguay-foreigners-2026.html` made the claim in its title area, meta, H2 and conclusion. Uruguay in fact taxes the gain on selling property at 12% (IRPF, art. 37-T7), which the Punta del Este page already corrected on Oct 22. The page also stated the pre-2026 "11-year tax holiday" and had no Sources section. It now gives the 12% rule, the Law 20.446 regime, sourced closing costs (2% ITP each side, notary ~3% + VAT, 5–9% total buyer costs) and 10 primary sources.
- **P0 (fixed): "2026 Global Investment Report" showed unsourced projections.** `/reports/2026-global-investment-report.html` is featured at the top of `/guides.html`.
  - It showed a "YoY Growth" column (Colombia 15–20%, Costa Rica 8–12%) and a "Best appreciation" pick. Projected appreciation is against PLAYBOOK rule 8.
  - It also showed an unsourced $687B → $1.27T market size, and Nicaragua yields of 9–13% while the same page said no reliable data exists.
  - Other errors: Playa del Carmen 8–11% Airbnb yields, Panama Friendly Nations described as "tax residency", and Costa Rica tax "capped at 0.25%".
  - The growth figures are removed. Prices and yields now come from the sourced city guides (n/a where none exists), with 28 primary links.
- **Publishing is alive.** Add blog/tip/quick-read commits shipped on 10-26 and Add guide on 10-23; the last Research: commit was 10-26.
- **The visitor count is mostly not people.** Last 7 days of counted views by arrival: Sec-Fetch-Site "none" 275, cross-site 40, same-origin 4. Real readers click between pages, so the "830 visitors" baseline is mostly headless browsers opening URLs directly. Judge growth by Search Console and search referrals (22 last week). The code PR stops counting direct /contact.html opens and adds a per-arrival breakdown (`via`).
