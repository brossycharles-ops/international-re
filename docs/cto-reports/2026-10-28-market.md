## Independent check
A separate checker opened every source for all 19 changed or re-confirmed values. All 19 were CONFIRMED, with no mismatches. It spot-checked 8 listings in each Nicaraguan sample. Fixes made after the check:
- San Juan del Sur cost of living was rounded down from $1,105 to $1,100.
- The Lima price now names the districts it covers.
- The Panama currency source note no longer claims more than trade.gov says.
- PanamaProp's lower 4 Oct 2026 figures were added to the source note. They still fall inside the range.

Caveats the checker raised:
- The Panama figures are asking or listing-based. There is no official price series, and GPG's $2,669 is for new builds.
- BCRP data covers only 4 upscale districts.
- The Nicaragua ranges are quartiles of our own listing samples. One San Juan del Sur listing's m² field conflicts with its own description.
- San Juan del Sur rents on Numbeo are about a year old.
