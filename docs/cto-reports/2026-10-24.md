# Site audit — 2026-10-24 (cloud auditor)

## Status (HEALTHY / ISSUES / P0 ALERT)
ISSUES. One P0 found and fixed today: the homepage ROI calculator gave misleading investment projections. The site is up, every sitemap URL returns 200, and every form delivers.

## Alerts
- **P0 (fixed):** the homepage ROI calculator (`public/lead_capture.js`, `public/index.html#roi-calculator`) showed a "Total Return" that compounded unsourced appreciation on top of the yield. Colombia was set to 18% a year, so $250,000 in Medellín "returned" $762,939 over 5 years. The yields also contradicted the sourced city guides. It now covers only the 13 cities whose guides cite a yield, uses the midpoint of the sourced range, and shows gross rental income only, labelled as excluding appreciation and costs. The same example now shows $86,250 of gross rent over 5 years. The unlock overlay never cleared after signup either; that is also fixed.
- **P1 (fixed):** the quiz results (`public/quiz.html`) showed the same unsourced "Growth 18%/yr" figures and yields such as Mexico 8–12% and Nicaragua 9–13%. Growth is removed, and yields and prices now use the sourced city-guide figures, or "n/a" where none is published. The result cards were also squashed into narrow columns on phones; that is fixed.
- **Publishing is alive.** The Growth Engine shipped Add guide/quick-read/tip commits on 10-20, 10-21, 10-22 and 10-23, and the Research Editor shipped a Research: commit on 10-23.
- **Signups:** 0 signups since 2026-10-18, with about 1,374 visitors over 28 days (0.2% conversion). Every form was tested and works, so this is a conversion problem, not a broken form.
