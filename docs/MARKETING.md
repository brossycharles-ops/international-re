# Growth strategy: more readers, more subscribers

Targets: **500 organic visitors/month** and steady weekly subscriber growth.
Measured every Monday in the weekly owner report (needs `PLAUSIBLE_API_KEY` for traffic).

## The funnel

| Stage | What brings people through | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | Long-tail search pages (1 main piece + quick-read + tip daily), 17 researched city guides, FAQ rich results, IndexNow pings, RSS → dlvr.it social posts | Automated (Growth Engine) |
| **Trust** | Sourced numbers, honest risk notes, verified photos, fresh dates | Automated quality queues |
| **Convert** | One clear offer everywhere — the free Expat Starter Kit; email-only forms; quiz; exit-intent on desktop only | Live |
| **Nurture** | Instant kit on the thank-you page, welcome email, 9-step drip (days 1–90), Monday newsletter | Live, server-run |
| **Refer** | Forward-to-a-friend line in every newsletter and the welcome email | Live |

## Why these changes
- The site promised a "38-page Starter Kit" on 183 pages that didn't exist. Broken promises kill
  repeat visits and word of mouth. The kit now exists and every offer matches it.
- Required first and last name fields on 146 article forms added friction. Only email is required now.
- Full-screen popups on phones hurt Google mobile rankings. They're desktop-only and delayed.
- Thin templated pages (64 landing pages) can drag down a whole site in search. They're noindexed;
  effort goes into researched city guides instead.
- 99% of articles cited no sources. The sources queue fixes one page a day; new pages always cite.

## Daily content engine (Growth Engine, 1pm ET)
Mon blog · Tue legal guide · Wed comparison · Thu strengthen weakest page · Fri city location guide ·
Sat news roundup · Sun refresh oldest page — plus a quick-read and a tip every day, 2 FAQ upgrades,
1 sourced-article upgrade, 1 researched city guide. Monday also writes the newsletter.

## Channels that need the owner (highest impact first)
1. **Google Search Console** (~10 min): verify the site, submit `sitemap.xml`. Fastest way to get
   daily pages indexed and see which searches find the site. Send the HTML verification tag to the
   agents and they will add it. Then add the site to **Bing Webmaster Tools** (it can import from Search Console).
2. **Pinterest business account** (~15 min): connect `https://www.internationalre.org/feed.xml` as an
   RSS feed so every new article becomes a pin automatically. Travel and expat property content does well there.
3. **dlvr.it**: confirm it is still connected to the RSS feed and posting to the social accounts it
   was set up for (it stopped while publishing was down June–October).
4. **Community posting (manual)**: `docs/outreach-templates.html` has drafts for Reddit and Facebook
   groups. Follow each group's self-promotion rules, answer the question first, and disclose that you
   run the site.
5. **Partnerships**: guest posts or link swaps with relocation lawyers, property managers and expat
   bloggers in the 11 markets. One relevant backlink is worth dozens of new pages.

## What to watch each week (Sunday review)
- Visitors (7d/30d) and top pages: double down on topics and formats that get traffic.
- New subscribers per week and which page they joined from.
- Queues remaining: FAQ, sources, cities.
- Any blocked publish or auditor P0.
