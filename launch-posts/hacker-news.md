## When to post

- **Best:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday, **6:30–8:00am PT** (9:30–11am ET). This is when HN traffic peaks.
- **Avoid:** Friday afternoon, weekends, US holidays.
- **Same-day rule:** Don't post any other HN submissions the same day (algorithm penalizes).

## Critical do's and don'ts

**DO:**
- Post from your own HN account (create one at https://news.ycombinator.com/login if needed — username will be visible). Accounts with ≥1 karma point can submit; new accounts work too.
- Reply to **every comment** in the first 2 hours. HN ranking weighs comment velocity heavily. Even hostile comments deserve a real reply.
- Be ready to update the post with new findings as commenters surface things.
- If someone says you're wrong about something, **say "you're right, let me check" and actually go check.** HN respects this enormously.

**DON'T:**
- Ask anyone to upvote. HN actively detects voting rings — auto-shadowbans accounts and submissions. This includes sharing the HN link on Twitter saying "check out my post."
- Post the link in your newsletter or to friends until after 6 hours. (You can share organically after — just don't solicit votes.)
- Use marketing language in any comment reply. HN hates it. Talk like a real person.
- Get defensive. The community will dunk on something. Take it well, learn from it.

## If you hit the front page

Traffic will be 5,000–50,000 visitors in 6-8 hours. Things that matter:
- Your subscribe modal is already live and fast — should hold up
- Plausible analytics will show you what they're clicking
- Stay near your computer for 2-3 hours after posting to reply to comments

## If you don't hit the front page

90% of submissions don't. If yours is in the bottom 90%:
- Wait 2-3 weeks. Submit a different URL (one of your specific country deep-dives, like the Buenos Aires post — its contrarian angle works for HN) with a different title.
- HN's algorithm de-ranks repeat submissions of the same URL, but a new URL on the same domain is fine.
