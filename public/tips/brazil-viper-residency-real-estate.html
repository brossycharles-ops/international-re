Brazil Investor Visa: R$700,000 Property in the Northeast (2026)

By Charles Brossy May 30, 2026 2 min read

Brazil's real-estate investor residence (often called VIPER) is open to foreigners who buy urban property worth at least R$1,000,000 with funds from abroad. The minimum can be up to 30% lower, R$700,000, in the North and Northeast regions (about US$130,000 at the Banco Central PTAX rate of R$5.37 per dollar on October 29, 2026). The rule is Normative Resolution 36/2018 of Brazil's National Immigration Council.

What the Investment Gets You

In the Northeast (Fortaleza, Natal and Recife, for example) a R$700,000 urban purchase meets the reduced threshold. Under the resolution:

  • A four-year residence permit comes first.
  • Residence of indefinite duration can follow if you still hold the investment and have spent at least 14 days in Brazil in every two-year period.
  • Several properties can be combined to reach the minimum; a cheaper apartment on its own gives no residence right.
  • Direct ownership: foreigners can hold urban property in their own name. See our guide to buying property in Brazil.

In the South, Southeast and Centre-West (São Paulo, Rio, Brasília), the full R$1,000,000 applies (about US$186,000 at the same rate).

Why Northeast Brazil?

Fortaleza and Natal sit on some of the most dramatic coastline in South America: wind-sculpted dunes, warm shallow lagoons, and sun most of the year. Property prices there are lower than in Rio or São Paulo, so a single condo can clear the reduced threshold. Rents and dollar values move with the real, so check current yields and the exchange rate before you buy.

The Bottom Line

If you're comparing residency-by-investment routes, Brazil's is one of the less known: R$700,000 of urban property in the North or Northeast, a four-year permit, then indefinite residence if you keep the investment and visit at least 14 days every two years.

Sources

  • Ministério da Justiça e Segurança Pública — Resolução Normativa nº 36/2018 (R$1,000,000 urban property, up to 30% lower in North/Northeast, several properties may be combined, four-year term, indefinite residence, 14 days every two years)
  • Banco Central do Brasil — SGS series 1, PTAX US dollar rate (R$5.3744 per US$ on October 29, 2026)
