Colombia Pension Visa Requirements 2026: A Pension of 3 Minimum Wages

By Charles Brossy October 21, 2026 1 min read

The key Colombia pension visa requirement in 2026 is a lifetime monthly pension of at least three legal minimum wages (SMMLV). With the 2026 minimum wage at COP 1,750,905 (Decree 1469 of 2025 was suspended by the Consejo de Estado in February 2026, and transitional Decree 0159 kept the same amount), that is COP 5,252,715 a month, about US$1,660 at the official TRM of COP 3,163.76 on October 21, 2026. The rule: Article 77 of Cancillería's Resolución 5477 of 2022.

What Else Cancillería Requires

  • Term: up to three years; no work allowed.
  • Path to residence: after five years as an M Pensionado holder, you can apply for the Resident (R) visa.
  • Documents: an apostilled, translated pension certificate (or one issued by the consulate of the country paying the pension), a criminal record check (last three years), a medical certificate and health coverage valid in Colombia.
  • Dependents: a dependent spouse or partner and children up to 25 (or with a disability).

The peso threshold rises with each year's minimum wage, so a dollar pension can fall short at renewal. The US State Department rates Colombia Level 3, “Reconsider Travel.”

Buying a home needs no visa: the Constitution (Art. 100) gives foreigners the same civil rights as Colombians. Property worth at least 350 SMMLV, registered with Banco de la República, is a separate Visa M Inversionista route.
