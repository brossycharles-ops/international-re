Panama Pensionado Visa: a $1,000 Pension, or $750 If You Own a $100,000 Home

By Charles Brossy October 22, 2026 1 min read

Panama's Pensionado visa needs a lifetime pension of at least B/.1,000 (US$1,000) a month. But if you bought Panamanian property for more than B/.100,000 in your own name, the minimum drops to B/.750 a month, according to the government's Panamá Digital procedures portal. The permit is permanent residency, not a temporary visa.

The portal requires the property to be held a título personal (in your own name), so check with your lawyer before buying through a company. Once resident, Law 6 of 1987 gives jubilados discounts such as 25% on airfares and 50% on cinema, theatre and other entertainment tickets. Electricity is 25% off on the first 600 kWh a month, and the utility requires the account to be in your name.

Sources

  • Panamá Digital — Solicitud de permiso de residente permanente como jubilado y pensionado (B/.1,000 minimum pension; B/.750 with personal property bought for over B/.100,000; permanent residence)
  • Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias — Ley 6 de 1987 (beneficios a jubilados) (25% airfares, 50% entertainment)
  • Caja de Seguro Social — Ley 37 de 2001 (G.O. 24340) (25% electricity discount on the first 600 kWh a month)
  • ENSA — Descuentos especiales (account must be in the pensioner's name)
