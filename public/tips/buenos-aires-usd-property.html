Market Stat: Buenos Aires Apartments Trade in USD at $2,400/sqm — 86% Below Manhattan

By Charles Brossy May 21, 2026 2 min read

Argentina is one of the only countries on earth where locals and foreigners alike buy real estate in US dollars. The citywide median sits at around $2,400/sqm USD — compared to over $17,000/sqm in Manhattan. One is the "Paris of South America." The other has the price tag to match.

Why Argentina Transacts in Dollars

Decades of peso devaluations taught Argentine sellers one lesson: never price an asset in local currency. Today, virtually every residential property in Buenos Aires is listed, negotiated, and closed in USD — a legal and cultural norm that's been in place for generations. For foreign buyers, this is genuinely unusual: there's no currency conversion risk baked into the purchase price the way there is in Mexico, Colombia, or Brazil.

The peso has lost over 97% of its value against the dollar since 2019. Property prices in USD, by contrast, fell modestly and are now recovering — transactions jumped 45% year-over-year in the first half of 2025.

What Your Dollars Buy

  • City median: ~$2,200–2,500/sqm — a quality 2BR in a livable neighborhood for $120,000–$180,000
  • Palermo & Recoleta: $3,500–4,300/sqm — Buenos Aires' most sought-after expat and dining districts
  • Puerto Madero: $5,000–6,500/sqm — the waterfront premium tier
  • Rental yields: 5–9% gross in high-demand neighborhoods for smaller apartments
  • Foreign ownership: Fully unrestricted on all urban property — buy in your own name, no trust or special structure required

The Bottom Line

Buenos Aires is a genuinely world-class city — European architecture, excellent healthcare, acclaimed food scene, and a large English-speaking expat community — at a price point that would be unthinkable in any comparable city. The dollar denominator removes a layer of risk that exists in most emerging markets. That combination is rare.
