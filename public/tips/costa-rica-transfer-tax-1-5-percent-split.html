Costa Rica Transfer Tax: 1.5%, Split 50/50 by Law

By Charles Brossy October 27, 2026 1 min read

Costa Rica's property transfer tax is 1.5% of the price in the deed, and by law the buyer and the seller pay it in equal parts: 0.75% each, with both jointly liable for the full amount (Law 6999, Articles 6 and 8). The taxable value can never be lower than the value the tax administration has on record for the property.

On top of the transfer tax, the National Registry charges a registration fee of 0.5% (¢5 per ¢1,000, minimum ¢2,000) under the Public Registry Fees Law, calculated on the higher of the value declared by the parties or the value in the Registro Único de Valores. On a US$300,000 house deeded at its full price, that is US$4,500 of transfer tax (US$2,250 per side) and US$1,500 of registration fee, before stamp duties and notary fees. Because both parties are jointly liable, check that the seller's half is paid at closing. If the seller is not resident in Costa Rica, the buyer must also withhold 2.5% of the price for the seller's capital-gains tax and pay it to Hacienda, a rule applied since October 2025, according to EY.   • eRegulations Costa Rica — Ley 4564, Aranceles del Registro Público (0.5% registration fee, ¢2,000 minimum, base rule (Art. 2.a–b))
