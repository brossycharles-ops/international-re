Panama Exempts the First $120,000 of a New Home From Its 2% Transfer Tax

By Charles Brossy October 30, 2026 1 min read

Panama's property transfer tax (ITBI) is 2% of the price, with the cadastral value as the minimum base, and the seller pays it under Law 106 of 1974. Since Law 546 (Official Gazette, August 31, 2026; in force September 1), the first sale of a new home is exempt on the first B/.120,000 (US$120,000) of that base, if the sale is formalized by public deed within two years of the occupancy permit.

  • B/.120,000 to B/.200,000: tax only on the part above B/.120,000, at a reduced rate of 0.5% to 1.8% depending on the price band.
  • Over B/.200,000: the first B/.120,000 stays exempt; the normal rate applies to the rest.
  • Buyer protection: the new-home article makes any contract clause that makes the buyer pay or reimburse the tax that legally falls on the seller void.
  • Resales are not covered: the exemption is only for the first sale of a new home. Homes with an earlier occupancy permit qualify if the sale is formalized within 30 months of it.

Sources

  • Gaceta Oficial 30601-B — Panama Law 546 of August 31, 2026 (La Prensa copy) (B/.120,000 exemption, 2-year window, 0.5%–1.8% scale up to B/.200,000, void clause, 30-month transitional rule)
  • Panama — Law 106 of 1974 (Justia copy of the gazette text) (2% rate, cadastral minimum, paid by the seller (Art. 1))
  • La Estrella de Panamá — Mulino sanciona ley que exonera ITBI en los primeros $120,000 de viviendas nuevas (law signed August 31, 2026; not a full exemption)
