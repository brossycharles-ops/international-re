Market Stat: Cartagena Beachfront Condos Sell for $3,500/sqm — 68% Less Than Miami Beach

By International RE May 14, 2026 2 min read

Bocagrande — Cartagena's main beachfront strip — averages $3,500 per square meter for a waterfront condo. Miami Beach averages north of $11,000/sqm. Both face the Caribbean or Atlantic. One costs 68% less.

The Numbers Behind the Gap

Cartagena sits on Colombia's Caribbean coast: 30°C year-round, white-sand beaches, UNESCO World Heritage walled city, and direct flights to Miami in under three hours. Yet its real estate market prices like a secret.

According to January 2026 data from TheLatinvestor, Bocagrande beachfront condos range from roughly $2,100/sqm on the low end to $7,500/sqm for premium oceanview units — averaging around $3,500/sqm. Miami Beach, where luxury condos now trade above $1,000/sqft ($10,760/sqm), is simply in a different league on price.

A 2-bedroom Bocagrande condo with sea views costs $200,000–$400,000 USD. The comparable unit in South Beach starts at $800,000 and runs past $2 million.

The Yield Story Is Just as Compelling

Cartagena's short-term rental market is one of Colombia's strongest:

  • Gross rental yields: 8–12% in prime Cartagena neighborhoods
  • Occupancy: Average 54% annually — peak season (December–March) runs considerably higher
  • Price appreciation: Cartagena property values rose ~8% in 2025, above Colombia's general inflation rate
  • Foreign ownership: No restrictions — foreigners buy freehold title in their own name

Colombia's peso has weakened meaningfully against the dollar over the past several years, giving USD buyers enhanced purchasing power that partially offsets any future appreciation the peso recaptures.

The Bottom Line

If you're drawn to Caribbean beachfront but priced out of Florida, Cartagena deserves a serious look. At $3,500/sqm for Bocagrande and 8–12% gross yields from short-term rentals, the fundamentals are hard to dismiss — and the city itself is genuinely world-class.
