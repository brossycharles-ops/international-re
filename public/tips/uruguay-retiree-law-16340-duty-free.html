Uruguay's Retiree Law: US$1,500 a Month and a US$100,000 Home Bring a Duty-Free Car

By Charles Brossy October 28, 2026 2 min read

Uruguay's retiree law (Law 16.340 of 1992) lets a foreign retiree or pensioner with US$1,500 a month of pension or other income from abroad, plus a home in Uruguay worth at least US$100,000, import their household goods and one vehicle free of customs duties. The home cannot be sold for ten years.

The Conditions

  • Income: at least US$1,500 a month from a pension or other income generated abroad (Art. 2 B).
  • Property or bonds: a home in Uruguay worth at least US$100,000 that you keep for ten years. Instead, you can hold at least US$100,000 face value of Uruguayan government securities in custody at the state bank, BROU, for ten years (Art. 2 C).
  • Benefits: furniture and household goods free of customs duties, and one vehicle that cannot be transferred for four years (Art. 3). Imports must be made within six months of permanent residence being granted (Decree 119/004).
  • Limits: beneficiaries may not take salaried employment without government authorization (Art. 5), and the vehicle needs liability insurance (Art. 6). The government lists the procedure as free.
