CONFOTUR: Up to 15 Years Without IPI for First Buyers

By Charles Brossy October 23, 2026 1 min read

Buy a unit directly from the developer in a CONFOTUR-classified tourism project in the Dominican Republic and that first purchase can be exempt from the 3% transfer tax. The DGII also exempts first buyers who are individuals from the annual IPI property tax for the rest of the project’s exemption period: 15 years counted from when construction is finished, not from when you buy. Later resales do not qualify.

The DGII’s tourism-sector guide lists the Law 158-01 exemptions, including transfer taxes and the IPI, and Article 7’s 15-year period; a project that does not start operating within three years loses the exemption. Law 195-13 extended the benefits to individuals and companies that invest directly with the developer, excluding any later transfer to third parties, and the DGII’s 2026 IPI guide lists properties under Law 158-01 belonging to first individual buyers as exempt. Note that individuals only owe the 1% IPI on combined holdings above RD$10,695,494 in 2026. Ask for the project’s classification resolution: Pellerano & Herrera warns that not every project or transfer is treated the same. Since June 2026, Law 30-26 also bars benefiting from more than one incentive regime for the same investment. Full details are in our Punta Cana investment guide; or take our market quiz.

Sources

  • DGII — Cartilla: Obligaciones y exenciones Sector Turismo (Law 158-01 Art. 4: transfer taxes and IPI; Art. 7: 15 years from completion of construction, three-year deadline to start operating)
  • Pellerano & Herrera — La Ley 158-01 de CONFOTUR bajo la Ley 30-26 (August 2026) (first transfer of units; Law 195-13; no-stacking rule; check the classification resolution)
  • DGII — Norma General 08-14 (3% transfer tax under Law 173-07)
  • DGII — Ley 195-13 (exemptions extended to investors buying directly from developers; later transfers to third parties excluded)
  • DGII — Guía 17: Impuesto al Patrimonio Inmobiliario (IPI) (Law 158-01 properties of first individual buyers exempt; 1% above RD$10,695,494 in 2026)
  • Infobae — Luis Abinader promulga la Ley 30-26 (June 2026) (Law 30-26 promulgated in June 2026)
