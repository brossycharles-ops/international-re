Market Stat: Playa del Carmen Condos Cost $3,800/sqm — 70% Less Than Miami Beach

By International RE May 23, 2026 2 min read

The average condo in Playa del Carmen, Mexico sells for around $3,800 per square meter. On Miami Beach, that same square meter costs over $13,000. Both front the same Caribbean Sea. The price gap is 70%.

Why the Riviera Maya Is Attracting Serious Buyers

Playa del Carmen sits at the heart of the Riviera Maya, a 130-kilometre stretch of white-sand Caribbean coastline south of Cancún. Once a quiet fishing village, it has grown into a walkable resort city with a world-class restaurant scene, reliable infrastructure, and direct flights from dozens of North American cities. Over 20 million tourists visit the region each year — and a growing slice of them are now buyers.

The investment case stacks up fast:

  • Entry prices: A quality 1–2 bedroom condo near the beach runs $150,000–$350,000 USD — well below comparable Caribbean markets
  • Short-term rental yields: Well-managed Airbnb condos in prime areas return 8–12% gross annually, driven by year-round tourist demand
  • Foreign ownership: Foreigners buy legally via a fideicomiso — a 50-year renewable bank trust that grants full ownership rights in coastal zones
  • Appreciation: Playa del Carmen condo prices have risen roughly 8–12% per year in nominal USD terms over the past several years

The peso's weakness against the dollar since 2022 has further amplified purchasing power for USD buyers — making the already-affordable entry point even more compelling.

The Bottom Line

If you want Caribbean beachfront and Miami Beach has priced you out, the math on Playa del Carmen is hard to dismiss. You get the same sea, similar climate, and strong rental demand — at roughly 30 cents on the dollar.
