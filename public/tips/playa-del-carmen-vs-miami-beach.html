Playa del Carmen vs Miami Beach: Apartments Cost About 60% Less per m²

By Charles Brossy May 23, 2026 · Updated October 23, 2026 3 min read

Apartments for sale in Playa del Carmen, Mexico had a median asking price of MXN 51,018 per square meter in October 2026, according to Propiedades.com: about US$2,730/m² at the Banxico FIX rate of 18.7005 pesos per dollar. In Miami Beach, Redfin's median sale price was US$632 per square foot (about US$6,800/m²) in the three months to September 2026. That makes Playa about 60% cheaper per m², not the "70% less" this tip used to claim.

Correction, October 23, 2026: this tip previously put Playa del Carmen at $3,800/m², Miami Beach at over $13,000/m² and Airbnb yields at 8–12%. Neither the citywide median nor the priciest neighbourhood we checked (Zazil-Ha, about $3,360/m²) reaches $3,800/m²; $13,000/m² (about $1,200/sq ft) is luxury oceanfront pricing, not the Miami Beach median; and we found no reliable source for 8–12% yields. We also said the peso had weakened since 2022. It has strengthened.

Playa's median listing in the same data asks MXN 3,979,370 (about US$213,000) for a median 78 m². Note that the Playa figures are asking prices and the Miami Beach figure is a sale price, so the real gap may be somewhat larger. Beachfront buildings in both places sell well above these medians.

What Else the Data Says

  • Rental yields: Global Property Guide does not publish a Playa del Carmen yield. Its nearest market, Cancún, averages 4.60% gross on long-term rentals (Q2 2026), against 5.79% for Mexico. We found no authoritative source for 8–12% Airbnb yields.
  • Price growth: Sociedad Hipotecaria Federal's appraisal-based index showed home values in Solidaridad, the municipality that includes Playa del Carmen, up 9.96% year on year in Q2 2026 (Quintana Roo: 9.80%), in pesos and across new and used homes of all types. The median listing price on Propiedades.com rose far less, about 2.7% between April 2025 and October 2026.
  • Ownership: Playa is inside Mexico's restricted zone (within 50 km of the coast), so foreigners buy residential property through a bank trust (fideicomiso) with a Foreign Affairs (SRE) permit. The trust lasts up to 50 years and can be extended; the bank holds title and you hold the use and benefit.
  • Short-term rental tax: Quintana Roo's lodging tax is 5% in general and 6% for private apartments, houses and villas, whether the host or a platform collects it, and hosts must register with the state tax registry.
  • Tourism: Quintana Roo as a whole received more than 21 million tourists in 2025.

The peso is about 7% stronger against the dollar than in 2022: the FIX averaged 20.12 that year and stood at 18.70 on October 22, 2026. Exchange-rate moves cut both ways for a dollar buyer, so don't count on them.

The Bottom Line

Playa del Carmen still costs well under Miami Beach per square meter, about 60% less on today's medians. But the case rests on price and lifestyle, not on double-digit rental yields that no authoritative source supports. The US State Department rates Mexico Level 2, "Exercise Increased Caution" (May 2026); read the full advisory before you buy.
