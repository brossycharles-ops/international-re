Buying Property in Tbilisi as a Foreigner in 2026: Taxes, Prices and Residency

By Charles Brossy Updated October 22, 2026 7 min read

Can foreigners buy property in Tbilisi, and what tax do they pay on the rent? Yes: foreigners can buy apartments and houses in Georgia freely (only agricultural land is off-limits), and in 2026 rent from a residential flat is taxed at 5% of gross rent, not the 1% “small business” rate many guides promise. Georgia's 1% regime explicitly excludes rental income. Earlier versions of this article got that wrong; this page has been corrected and re-sourced as of October 22, 2026.

The Tax on Rental Income: 5%, Not 1%

Georgia's small-business status taxes turnover at 1% up to 500,000 GEL a year (about US$192,000 at the National Bank of Georgia rate of 2.5978 GEL per dollar on October 21, 2026). But Andersen Georgia's 2026 guide to the regime states that some income is always taxed at the standard 20% regardless of small-business status, and rental income is on that list. What makes Tbilisi attractive instead is the special 5% rate: PwC's Georgia tax summary (reviewed September 2026) says an individual renting residential space for residential purposes can pay 5% personal income tax on the gross rent, with no deductions. PwC adds that a non-resident whose tenant is not a tax agent is taxed under the general rules instead, so confirm your position with a Georgian adviser.

At Galt & Taggart's city-average rent of US$10.1 per m² a month, a 55 m² flat brings in about US$555 a month, or US$6,660 a year gross, and about US$333 in tax at 5%. Sale of residential property is exempt from tax if you have owned it for more than two years, according to Andersen; a shorter hold can be taxed at 5%.

Property Tax Depends on Family Income

Georgian property tax for individuals is triggered by household income, not by the value of the flat. Property other than land is exempt when the family's annual income does not exceed 40,000 GEL. Above that, the tax is charged on market value at the end of the year: in Tbilisi, 0.2% for families earning under 100,000 GEL and 0.8% for families above it. Returns are due by November 1 and payment by November 15. A foreign family with income above 100,000 GEL holding a US$200,000 flat should budget about US$1,600 a year.

What Tbilisi Apartments Cost in 2026

Galt & Taggart's June 2026 Tbilisi report puts the average new-build price at US$1,417 per m² and newer resale flats at US$1,345 per m². Outer districts such as Didi Digomi and Gldani sit around US$1,150 or below, while Vake and Mtatsminda exceed US$2,800–2,900 per m².

How Foreigners Buy

The Chambers 2026 real estate guide confirms that foreigners may acquire non-agricultural real estate without any residency or reciprocity requirement, while foreign individuals are generally prohibited from owning agricultural land. Georgia levies no separate transfer tax. Registration at the National Agency of Public Registry costs 150 GEL for a four-working-day service, 270 GEL for one day or 350 GEL for same-day registration. Budget separately for a notary, a lawyer and any broker; we have not found a reliable published figure for total closing costs.

Residency Through Property: Now US$150,000

From March 1, 2026, the property threshold for a short-term residence permit rose from US$100,000 to US$150,000 of market value (non-agricultural property, assessed by an accredited valuer), according to EIG Law and Georgia's Public Service Development Agency. The permit is renewed annually. Permanent residence normally needs ten years of temporary residence; a separate investment permit for property worth more than US$300,000 offers a faster route. Without any permit, nationals of more than 90 countries, including the US, UK, Canada and Australia, can stay visa-free for up to a year, and since January 1, 2026 visitors must carry health and accident insurance of at least 30,000 GEL.

The Risks, Honestly

  • Politics: in November 2024 the government said it would not pursue EU accession talks before the end of 2028, prompting large protests, and a stricter foreign-agents law with criminal penalties took effect on May 31, 2025. Georgia's EU path is effectively frozen.
  • Security: the US State Department rates Georgia Level 1, “Exercise Normal Precautions,” but says do not travel to the Russian-occupied regions of Abkhazia and South Ossetia.
  • Liquidity and data: yields are gross asking-rent estimates, short-term rental returns are not published reliably, and resale times vary widely, so underwrite conservatively.
  • Currency: deals are usually quoted in dollars but the lari floats, and the 500,000 GEL and 40,000 GEL thresholds move in dollar terms with it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners buy property in Tbilisi?

Yes. Foreigners can buy apartments, houses and commercial property in Georgia without residency or reciprocity requirements. Only agricultural land is generally closed to foreign individuals.

What tax do foreigners pay on rental income in Georgia?

Rent from residential property let for residential purposes can be taxed at 5% of gross rent. Rental income does not qualify for the 1% small-business regime; otherwise the standard rate is 20%.

How much property do I need to buy for Georgian residency?

Since March 1, 2026, non-agricultural property with a market value above US$150,000 qualifies for a renewable short-term residence permit. An investment permit for property above US$300,000 is a separate route.
