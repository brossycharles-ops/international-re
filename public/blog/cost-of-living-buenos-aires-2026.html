Cost of Living in Buenos Aires 2026: Real Numbers for Expats

Updated October 30, 2026 · 7 min read · By Charles Brossy

The cost of living in Buenos Aires in 2026 for a single expat is roughly US$1,380 to US$1,610 a month, rent included: Numbeo's data, updated on October 28, 2026, puts a single person's monthly costs at about US$882 before rent, and a one-bedroom flat at about US$731 in the city centre or US$494 outside it. A family of four spends about US$3,147 a month before rent. Those dollar figures depend on the exchange rate, which the Banco Central put at ARS 1,539 per US dollar on October 29, 2026, and on inflation, which was 33.4% over the 12 months to September.

Why dollar costs keep moving: prices in Buenos Aires are set in pesos. Monthly inflation was 1.9% in September 2026 and prices rose 23.6% between January and September, while the official dollar moved from ARS 1,525 on October 20 to ARS 1,539 on October 29. When local prices rise faster than the peso weakens, life gets more expensive in dollars, so treat every figure on this page as a snapshot and recheck it before you move.

These are crowd-sourced averages, not listings, so check current asking rents in the barrio you want. Since DNU 70/2023 repealed the 2020 rental law (Law 27.551), landlord and tenant can agree the rent in pesos or in a foreign currency, choose any adjustment index and set the term; if they set no term, a home lease defaults to two years, and a tenant can leave early by paying 10% of the rent still due. Because the rules come from a decree, take local legal advice before signing a long lease. Health Care for Foreigners

Under Decree 366/2025 and Health Ministry Resolution 1066/2026, in force since August 11, 2026, foreigners without permanent residence must pay for non-urgent care (consultations, tests, planned surgery and treatment) at hospitals run by the national government, either through their health insurer or upfront against a quote. Emergencies must still be treated regardless of status or ability to pay, and permanent residents keep equal access with citizens. The rule covers hospitals run by the national government; it does not apply to provincial or city hospitals. For most newcomers that means budgeting for private cover from day one; prices depend on age and plan, so get quotes. Our expat health insurance guide compares the options.

The Real Risk: Inflation and the Peso

Argentina's costs can change fast. Even after inflation slowed, prices rose 33.4% in the 12 months to September 2026. If you live on dollars:

  • Re-check your budget every few months; a dollar figure from last year may be out of date.
  • Read the currency and adjustment clause in any lease before you sign.
  • Keep an emergency fund outside Argentina.
  • Arrange health insurance before you arrive if you will not hold permanent residence.

Is Buenos Aires Right for You?

It suits people with income from abroad who want a large, walkable city with cheap public transport and wine and beef at low prices, and who are comfortable with prices that change month to month. It is a harder fit if you need predictable costs, rely on free public health care without permanent residence, or plan to live on a fixed budget for years. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to live in Buenos Aires per month in 2026?

Numbeo's October 2026 data puts a single person's monthly costs at about US$882 before rent. Add a one-bedroom flat at about US$731 in the city centre or US$494 outside it, and a single person needs roughly US$1,380 to US$1,610 a month. A family of four needs about US$3,147 before rent.

How much is rent in Buenos Aires in 2026?

Numbeo's October 2026 averages are about US$731 a month for a one-bedroom flat in the city centre and US$494 outside the centre, and about US$1,466 and US$1,135 for three bedrooms. Leases can be agreed in pesos or US dollars under DNU 70/2023, which repealed the 2020 rental law.

Is the peso still losing value in 2026?

Yes, but more slowly than before. INDEC put inflation at 1.9% in September 2026 and 33.4% over 12 months. The Banco Central's reference rate was ARS 1,539 per US dollar on October 29, 2026, so dollar costs move with both the exchange rate and local prices.
