Latin America City Real Estate Guides

17 markets across 9 countries. Each guide aggregates our market analysis, legal guides, rental-yield data, and cost-of-living breakdowns for foreign buyers.

🇨🇴 Colombia

Medellín

Latin America's smartest value play — apartments from $1,500/sqm and 8,300 digital nomads arriving monthly.
🇨🇴 Colombia

Cartagena

Walled colonial beauty on the Caribbean with strong Airbnb demand and beachfront condos 68% below Miami.
🇵🇦 Panama

Panama City

Dollar economy, world-class skyline, and the most generous residency program in the Americas.
🇨🇷 Costa Rica

San José

Central Valley capital with deep expat infrastructure and political stability since 1948.

Guanacaste

Pacific surf-and-sun region — LIR airport's busiest quarter ever and a $1.2B development pipeline.

Buenos Aires

World-class architecture at $2,800/m². Capital controls gone, UVA mortgages back, and 7%+ gross yields.

Mexico City

Latin America's biggest economy hub — Polanco, Roma, Condesa drawing waves of remote workers.
🇲🇽 Mexico

Playa del Carmen

Riviera Maya's beach-town hub — fideicomiso ownership, dollar income from Airbnb tourists.
🇲🇽 Mexico

Tulum

High-yield jungle-beach market with the strongest STR income per dollar in Latin America.
🇨🇱 Chile

Santiago

Andean capital with the most stable peso in Latin America and supply-constrained luxury market.
🇺🇾 Uruguay

Montevideo

Latin America's most stable democracy — 10-year zero-tax holiday on foreign income for new residents.

Punta del Este

South America's Hamptons — peak-season rentals, year-round capital appreciation, and Mediterranean climate.

Lima

São Paulo
