Property Tax in Costa Rica for Foreigners (2026 Rates)

By Charles Brossy October 23, 2026 3 min read

Property tax in Costa Rica for foreigners works exactly as it does for citizens, because Law 7509 makes the registered owner the taxpayer regardless of nationality: an annual municipal tax of 0.25% of the property’s registered value under Law 7509. Owners of higher-value homes also pay the national Impuesto Solidario (“luxury home tax”) when the fiscal value of the construction exceeds ¢143 million in 2026, at rates from 0.25% to 0.55%.

The Municipal Tax: 0.25% a Year

Law 7509 taxes land plus fixed, permanent buildings. The rate is 0.25% nationwide, applied to the value registered by the municipality where the property sits, and the owner with title registered in the Registro Nacional is the taxpayer. Each municipality collects it annually, semi-annually or in four quarterly installments. Owners must declare the value of their property to the municipality at least every five years; if you don’t, the municipality can value it itself. On a home registered at ¢150,000,000, the tax is ¢375,000 a year.

The Luxury Home Tax (Impuesto Solidario) in 2026

Decree 45358-H, published on 19 December 2025, set the 2026 threshold at ¢143,000,000, about US$313,000 at the Banco Central’s 23 October 2026 reference selling rate of ¢456.32. The test is the fiscal value of the construction and fixed installations; if it is above the threshold, the land value is added to the taxable base. Rates on that fiscal value:

Fiscal value bandRate
Up to ¢359 million0.25%
¢359–720 million0.30%
¢720 million–1.081 billion0.35%
¢1.081–1.443 billion0.40%
¢1.443–1.800 billion0.45%
¢1.800–2.162 billion0.50%
Above ¢2.162 billion0.55%

The 2026 payment was due by 15 January 2026 through the Hacienda’s TRIBU-CR platform. The declaration is filed every three years, and it covers rental and recreational homes as well as your main residence. A bill filed in October 2025 (expediente 25.239) proposed raising the property tax rate to 0.75%, with municipalities keeping 0.25% and the extra going to the central government; it is a proposal, so check its status before you budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is property tax in Costa Rica for foreigners?

The same as for Costa Ricans: 0.25% a year of the value registered by the municipality, under Law 7509. On a home registered at 150 million colones, that is 375,000 colones a year.

What is the luxury home tax in Costa Rica in 2026?

The Impuesto Solidario applies when the fiscal value of a home's construction exceeds 143 million colones in 2026 (Decree 45358-H). Rates on the fiscal value run from 0.25% up to 359 million colones to 0.55% above 2.162 billion colones. The 2026 payment was due by 15 January 2026.

Do I have to declare my property value in Costa Rica?

Yes. Law 7509 requires owners to declare the value of their property to the municipality at least every five years. Owners subject to the luxury home tax also file a declaration with the Hacienda every three years through TRIBU-CR.
