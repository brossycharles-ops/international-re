Cost of Living in Cuenca Ecuador Per Month (2026)

By Charles Brossy October 20, 2026 3 min read

The cost of living in Cuenca, Ecuador per month is about $498 for a single person before rent, according to Numbeo’s crowd-sourced estimates (accessed October 20, 2026). Add a one-bedroom in the city centre at roughly $537 and a solo budget lands near $1,035; a family of four spends about $1,845 before rent. Everything is priced in US dollars, Ecuador’s currency since January 2000, according to the Banco Central del Ecuador.

Retiree Visa Income and Health Insurance

Ecuador’s retiree (jubilado) residence visa requires a pension of at least three salarios básicos unificados (SBU), per gob.ec. With the 2026 SBU at $482 (Ministerio del Trabajo), that is $1,446 a month, plus health insurance valid for the visa period. Fees: $50 to apply, $270 on approval, 50% off at 65+. See Retire in Ecuador 2026. To see how that compares with Colombia, read Cuenca vs Medellín for retirees.

Safety

The US State Department rates Ecuador Level 2, “Exercise Increased Caution” (August 18, 2026). Cuenca and Azuay are not on its restricted-area list, but it calls crime widespread nationwide. Unsure Cuenca fits? Take our market quiz.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I need per month to live in Cuenca, Ecuador?

Roughly $885 to $1,035 for a single person: Numbeo estimates about $498 before rent, plus a one-bedroom at about $389 outside the centre or $537 downtown.

What income do I need for Ecuador's retiree visa in 2026?

A pension of at least three SBU, or $1,446 a month at the 2026 SBU of $482, plus health insurance valid for the visa period.

Is Cuenca safe for expats?

The US State Department rates Ecuador Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution. Cuenca is not on its restricted-area list, but crime is widespread nationwide.
