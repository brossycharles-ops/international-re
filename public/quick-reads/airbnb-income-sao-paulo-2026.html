Airbnb Income in São Paulo 2026: Median, Rules and Tax

By Charles Brossy May 18, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026 3 min read

How much does an Airbnb earn in São Paulo in 2026? Airbtics puts the median listing at about R$60,000 a year in gross revenue (September 2025 to August 2026), with a 58% occupancy rate and an average nightly rate of R$270 (about US$53) across 35,986 active listings. That works out to roughly R$5,000 a month before platform fees, condominium charges and tax. Another provider, AirROI, measures a lower median of about US$625 a month at 40% occupancy, so treat any single number as a rough guide.

Two Data Sets, Two Answers

Short-term rental data companies scrape listings and estimate bookings, and their methods differ. For the 12 months to September 2026, AirROI shows a median of about US$625 a month, US$1,050 or more for the top 25% of listings, a US$60 average nightly rate and 40% occupancy. Airbtics' higher occupancy (58%) explains most of the gap. Use one provider's numbers consistently when you model a purchase, and test your plan at the lower figure.

For comparison, the FipeZAP index estimates São Paulo's gross yield on long-term rentals at 6.43% a year (September 2026), based on asking rents and asking prices. FipeZAP puts the average asking price at R$12,206 per m² citywide and R$18,028 per m² in Jardins.

Can Your Building Ban Airbnb?

Often, yes, and this is the main risk to check before buying. Brazil's Superior Court of Justice (STJ) has held that a condominium convention setting residential use can bar short stays booked through platforms. In 2026 it took up a binding nationwide case (Tema 1.443) and suspended related cases until it rules; changing a building's use requires the approval of two-thirds of owners. São Paulo also bans short-term rental of social-housing units (HIS/HMP), and in 2026 Airbnb removed 1,625 such listings at the city's request. Brazil's tenancy law itself allows "temporada" rentals of up to 90 days, with rent payable in advance.

Buying and Tax Basics for Foreigners

Foreigners can buy urban apartments in Brazil; the acquisition limits in Law 5.709/1971 apply to rural land. São Paulo charges 3% ITBI transfer tax on the higher of the price and the city's reference value (valor venal de referência), plus notary and registry fees, so ask the notary for a quote. Rent paid to a non-resident owner has 15% income tax withheld at source by the local agent (25% for owners in low-tax jurisdictions). Residents pay monthly through carnê-leão at progressive rates up to 27.5%. Under the 2026 tax reform, an individual only becomes liable for the new IBS/CBS taxes on rent with more than three rented properties and more than R$240,000 a year in rent.

Remember that property and rents are priced in reais: if the real weakens, both your rental income and your resale value fall in dollar terms. For prices, neighborhoods and residency, see our São Paulo real estate guide and the São Paulo city guide, and compare with Airbnb income in Mexico City.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a typical São Paulo Airbnb earn?

Data providers disagree. Airbtics puts the median listing at about R$60,000 a year, with 58% occupancy and an average nightly rate of R$270 (about US$53), for September 2025 to August 2026. AirROI's 12 months to September 2026 show a median of about US$625 a month and US$1,050+ a month for the top 25%. Both are gross figures, before fees, condominium charges and tax.

Can my building stop me renting on Airbnb in São Paulo?

Yes. Brazil's Superior Court of Justice (STJ) has held that a condominium convention setting residential use can bar short stays booked through platforms, and in 2026 it took up a binding nationwide case (Tema 1.443) and suspended related cases until it rules. Separately, São Paulo bans short-term rental of HIS/HMP social-housing units. Read the convention before you buy.

How is Airbnb income in Brazil taxed for foreigners?

Rent paid to a non-resident owner has 15% income tax withheld at source by the local agent (25% if the owner lives in a low-tax jurisdiction). Residents pay monthly through carnê-leão under the progressive table, which tops out at 27.5% in 2026. Under the 2026 tax reform, an individual only becomes liable for the new IBS/CBS taxes on rent with more than three rented properties and more than R$240,000 a year in rent.
