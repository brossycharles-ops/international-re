Property Tax in Chile for Foreigners: 2026 Rates and a Worked Example

By Charles Brossy October 19, 2026 3 min read

Property tax for foreigners in Chile works exactly as it does for Chileans: you pay contribuciones de bienes raíces, an annual tax set by the tax agency (SII) on the property’s fiscal appraisal (avalúo fiscal), not its market price. For homes in the second half of 2026, the first CLP 61,711,570 of appraisal is exempt, and the rest is taxed at 0.893% a year, rising to 1.042% on appraisal above CLP 220,398,431.

The 2026 Numbers

  • Residential exemption: CLP 61,711,570 (from 1 July 2026, after a 1.028 inflation adjustment)
  • Rate 1: 0.893% on the part of the appraisal between the exemption and CLP 220,398,431
  • Rate 2: 1.042% on the part of the appraisal above CLP 220,398,431
  • Non-residential property (offices, shops): 1.042%, generally with no exemption

Worked Example: A Home Appraised at CLP 150 Million

The SII’s formula is taxable appraisal = total appraisal − exempt amount. So CLP 150,000,000 − CLP 61,711,570 = CLP 88,288,430, and 0.893% of that is about CLP 788,416 a year in net tax, or about CLP 197,104 per quarterly installment. Check the appraisal on the SII’s certificate (certificado de avalúo fiscal) for the exact property before you buy, because the bill depends on that figure.

Paying It From Abroad

The SII bills contribuciones in four installments, in April, June, September and November, payable online on the SII or TGR (Treasury) websites, or in person at TGR payment points. Separate adjustment bills can arrive in June and December. The SII says the owner is liable for the tax, so have your lawyer confirm every installment on the property’s tax roll is paid up before closing. Owners whose combined appraisals exceed 827 UTA (about CLP 670 million, according to the SII) across all the properties they own also pay a separate sobretasa (surcharge). Frequently Asked Questions

Do foreigners pay higher property tax in Chile?

No. Chile's property tax (contribuciones) is set by the SII from a property's fiscal appraisal and use; the published exemption amounts and rates contain no separate rate for foreign or non-resident owners.

How much is property tax on a Chilean apartment in 2026?

For a home, the SII exempts the first CLP 61,711,570 of fiscal value (second half of 2026), then charges 0.893% a year on the part of the appraisal between the exemption and CLP 220,398,431, and 1.042% on the part above CLP 220,398,431. A home appraised at CLP 150 million pays about CLP 788,416 a year in net tax.

When are contribuciones due in Chile?

In four installments, in April, June, September and November. You can pay online on the SII or TGR (Treasury) websites, or in person at TGR payment points.
