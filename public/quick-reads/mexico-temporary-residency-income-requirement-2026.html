Mexico Temporary Residency Income Requirement 2026

By Charles Brossy October 21, 2026 3 min read

The Mexico temporary residency income requirement for 2026 is after-tax income above 680 UMA days a month for the last six months, or an average monthly balance of 11,460 UMA days over 12 months, under visa rules published in the Diario Oficial on July 25, 2025. At the 2026 UMA of MXN 117.31, that is about MXN 79,771 a month or MXN 1.34 million in savings. Retirees and remote workers use the same route.

What Consulates Actually Ask

Dollar figures vary by consulate and exchange rate. San Diego’s 2026 sheet asks for $4,049 a month or a $68,241 average balance for temporary residence (fee $56). Washington lists MXN 83,640 a month or MXN 1,394,000, which equals 300 and 5,000 days of the 2025 minimum wage (MXN 278.80), the older formula rather than the 2025 UMA multiples. Remote workers at San Diego need an employer letter approving remote work from Mexico.

Process and Risks

Book a consulate interview at citas.sre.gob.mx. The visa allows one entry within 180 days; exchange it for a resident card at INM within 30 calendar days of arrival. Buying a home is a separate process: see can foreigners buy property in Mexico and property tax for foreigners. The US State Department rates Mexico Level 2 (May 29, 2026), with some states higher. Frequently Asked Questions

How much income do I need for Mexican temporary residency in 2026?

About MXN 79,771 a month (680 UMA days) for six months, or 11,460 UMA days in average savings over 12 months. San Diego quotes $4,049 or $68,241.

Can remote workers use the economic solvency visa?

Yes, if salary meets the threshold. San Diego asks for an employer letter stating salary and approving remote work from Mexico.

What happens after the consulate issues the visa?

It allows one entry within 180 days. After arriving, exchange it for a resident card at the National Migration Institute (INM) within 30 calendar days.
