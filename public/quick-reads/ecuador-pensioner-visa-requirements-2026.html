Ecuador Pensioner Visa Requirements 2026: $1,446 a Month

By Charles Brossy October 22, 2026 3 min read

The core Ecuador pensioner visa requirement in 2026 is a monthly pension from abroad of at least three Salarios Básicos Unificados (SBU). With the 2026 SBU set at US$482 by Acuerdo Ministerial MDT-2025-195, that is US$1,446 a month, plus US$250 a month for each dependent. The Residencia Temporal de Jubilado is issued by Ecuador's Foreign Ministry (Cancillería) for two years.

What the Foreign Ministry Asks For

  • Pension proof: an official document from the paying institution confirming the monthly amount, apostilled (or legalized if the country is outside the Apostille Convention).
  • Criminal record: from your home country or wherever you lived in the last five years, translated and apostilled, issued no more than 180 days before your last entry; applicants over 18 only.
  • Health insurance: national or foreign cover valid for the whole visa period.
  • Passport valid for at least six months, and a recent photo.

Fees and the 2026 Numbers

The official fee is US$50 to apply and US$270 when the visa is granted, US$320 in total. Applicants aged 65 or over get a 50% discount. Because the threshold is tied to the SBU, it rose from US$1,410 in 2025 (when the SBU was US$470) and changes whenever the basic salary is updated.

Buying Property Is a Separate Route

A property purchase can itself qualify you: the investor temporary residence asks for an investment of at least 100 SBU (US$48,200 in 2026), which can be a registered property in your name. Ecuador is dollarized, but the US State Department rates it Level 2, “Exercise Increased Caution,” with Level 4 “Do Not Travel” zones in parts of Guayaquil and several other cantons. Frequently Asked Questions

How much income do I need for Ecuador's retirement visa in 2026?

At least three basic salaries (SBU) a month in pension income: US$1,446 in 2026, since the SBU is US$482. Each dependent adds US$250 a month.

How much does the Ecuador pensioner visa cost?

US$50 to apply and US$270 when it is granted, US$320 in total, according to Ecuador's Foreign Ministry. Applicants aged 65 or over get a 50% discount.

Can I get residency in Ecuador by buying property?

Yes, through the separate investor temporary residence: a registered property in your name worth at least 100 SBU, which is US$48,200 in 2026.
