Best Neighborhoods in Medellín for Expats (2026)

By Charles Brossy Updated October 28, 2026 5 min read

The best neighborhoods in Medellín for expats are El Poblado, Laureles and Envigado, and in 2026 they are no longer cheap: asking prices for used apartments run about COP 6.6 million per m² in Laureles (≈US$2,100) and COP 8.7 million in El Poblado (≈US$2,770), with Envigado in between or above depending on the building. Here is how the three compare on price, rent and daily life, with every figure sourced.

Dollar figures use the official exchange rate (TRM) of COP 3,148.02 per US dollar for October 28, 2026. Portal figures are asking prices, not closing prices, so treat them as a ceiling for negotiation.

El Poblado: The International Hub

El Poblado is where many newcomers start. Expat.com estimates that 25–30% of Medellín's foreign residents live there. That is a share of the city's foreigners, not of the neighborhood: the Alcaldía projects El Poblado's 2026 population at about 114,000 people.

  • Buy: median asking price for used apartments of COP 8,724,118 per m² (≈US$2,770), across 5,671 listings (PropFound, October 20, 2026). Ciencuadras puts premium El Poblado between COP 8.5 and 12 million per m² (≈US$2,700–3,810).
  • Rent: PropFound's estimated rent of COP 55,625 per m² a month implies about COP 2.8 million (≈US$880) for a 50 m² apartment.
  • Gross yield: PropFound's reference gross yield is 7.7%, before vacancy, management, taxes and building fees.

Laureles: The Local Favorite

Time Out ranked Laureles the world's coolest neighborhood in October 2023. PropFound's figures below cover the wider Laureles-Estadio area.

  • Buy: median asking price of COP 6,615,174 per m² (≈US$2,100) across 3,702 listings (PropFound, October 20, 2026).
  • Rent: an estimated COP 40,000 per m² a month, or about COP 2 million (≈US$635) for 50 m².
  • Gross yield: 7.3% reference gross yield (PropFound).

Envigado: Quieter, but Not Cheaper

Envigado is a separate municipality on El Poblado's southern border, connected by Metro Line A at the Envigado and Ayurá stations. Metrocuadrado's August 2026 data shows it is not cheaper than Medellín for mid-size apartments. Apartments of 50–100 m² averaged COP 7.97 million per m² in strata 3–4 (≈US$2,530) and COP 9.29 million in strata 5–6 (≈US$2,950). That compares with COP 6.50 million and 8.92 million in Medellín itself. Small units under 50 m² in strata 5–6 averaged COP 16.5 million per m².

What the Strata Mean for Your Bills

Colombian homes are classified into six socioeconomic strata, from 1 (low-low) to 6 (high). Strata 1–3 receive utility subsidies, stratum 4 pays the cost price, and strata 5–6 pay a surcharge (DANE). Check a listing's stratum before you buy: it changes your monthly utility bills.

Market Direction and Risk

Metrocuadrado data reported in April 2026 showed used homes in Medellín gaining about 9% in value in 2025, in pesos, which is not the same as a dollar return. Rents in the Aburrá Valley rose 7.1% on average through July 2026 (Publimetro). The US State Department rates Colombia Level 3, "Reconsider travel", with Level 4 reserved for specific departments that do not include Antioquia. Read the advisory before you go, and use a Colombian lawyer to check the title certificate (certificado de tradición y libertad) before you sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best neighborhood in Medellín for expats?

It depends on your budget and lifestyle. El Poblado has the most international services and the highest prices (median asking price about COP 8.7 million per m² in October 2026). Laureles is cheaper at about COP 6.6 million per m², and mid-size apartments in Envigado averaged COP 7.97–9.29 million per m² in August 2026.

How much does an apartment cost per m² in El Poblado in 2026?

PropFound's October 2026 median asking price for used apartments in El Poblado is COP 8,724,118 per m², about US$2,770 at the October 28, 2026 TRM. Ciencuadras puts premium El Poblado at COP 8.5–12 million per m².

Is Envigado cheaper than El Poblado?

Not for most apartments. Metrocuadrado's August 2026 data shows 50–100 m² apartments in Envigado averaging COP 7.97 million per m² in strata 3–4 and COP 9.29 million in strata 5–6, above Medellín's averages of COP 6.50 million and 8.92 million.
