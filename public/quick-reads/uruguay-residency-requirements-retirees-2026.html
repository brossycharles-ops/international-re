Uruguay Residency Requirements for Retirees (2026)

By Charles Brossy October 26, 2026 3 min read

Uruguay residency requirements for retirees in 2026 are, for most people, those of ordinary permanent legal residence, and there is no fixed income minimum. The Dirección Nacional de Migración asks a retiree or pensioner paid from abroad for a notary's certificate stating that status, the source of the income, the nominal monthly amount and how it reaches Uruguay; the Foreign Ministry says income must be “sufficient for maintenance.” The often-quoted US$1,500 a month belongs to a separate, optional Law 16.340 retiree regime that also requires a US$100,000 home or bond holding.

What Migración Asks For

  • Valid identity document (passport), plus a visa if your nationality needs one.
  • National criminal record certificate (applicants 18 and over; state records are not accepted) from your country of origin and every country where you lived six months or more in the last five years.
  • Carné de salud and vaccination certificate from health providers licensed by Uruguay's Ministry of Public Health.
  • Proof of means: the notary's certificate described above.
  • Birth certificate, required to issue your cédula (ID card).

Foreign documents must be apostilled or legalized and, where needed, translated by a Uruguayan public translator (documents issued by Brazil are exempt from translation).

Where You Apply, Cost and Timing

You start online on gub.uy, then attend an appointment at a Migración office in Uruguay. The fee is 557.30 indexed units (UI), plus 55.70 UI for the certificate used to obtain the cédula; arrive over 10 minutes late and the fee is lost. Migración publishes no processing time. Temporary residence (six months to two years, renewable) is for work, study and similar purposes.

The Law 16.340 Retiree Regime

This optional route, which starts at the nearest Uruguayan consulate (or at Migración if you are already in Uruguay as a temporary resident), requires at least US$1,500 a month in pension or other foreign income received regularly and permanently, plus a home worth at least US$100,000 kept for 10 years, or US$100,000 in Uruguayan government bonds held at BROU for 10 years. In return you may import household goods and one car duty-free (the car cannot be sold for four years); salaried employment is barred without Executive approval. Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum income for retiree residency in Uruguay?

Not for ordinary permanent residence: you certify your monthly income through a notary and it must be sufficient for maintenance, but no amount is published. US$1,500 a month applies only to the optional Law 16.340 regime.

Can I apply for Uruguayan residency from abroad?

Ordinary permanent residence starts online on gub.uy but requires an in-person appointment at a Migración office in Uruguay. The Law 16.340 regime starts at the nearest Uruguayan consulate, or at Migración if you are already in Uruguay as a temporary resident.

How much does Uruguay residency cost?

Migración lists 557.30 indexed units (UI), plus 55.70 UI for the cédula certificate and 225.60 UI for each re-entry permit if you leave the country while your application is pending; these UI-based fees are readjusted every six months. Notary, translation and apostille costs are extra.
