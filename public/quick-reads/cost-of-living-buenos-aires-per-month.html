Cost of Living in Buenos Aires Per Month (2026)

By Charles Brossy June 10, 2026 · Updated October 18, 2026 5 min read

A single person living in a central Buenos Aires neighbourhood such as Palermo or Belgrano should budget roughly $1,200–$1,900 a month in October 2026, including rent. Numbeo's crowd-sourced data puts a single person's costs at about $881 a month before rent, and Zonaprop's September 2026 index puts the average one-bedroom rent at about $585. After two years of peso stability, dollar prices are well above the levels many guides still quote, so use current figures.

All dollar figures below use the official retail rate of about ARS 1,535 per US dollar (Banco Nación, 15 October 2026). Peso prices rise most months, so treat the dollar figures as a snapshot.

The Currency Reality

The peso has been fairly stable in 2026. It floats inside a central-bank band, and in mid-October the official and informal ("blue") rates were within about 1% of each other. Most long-term rentals are priced in pesos, not dollars: an IDECBA study of Argenprop listings found 27.6% of Buenos Aires rental apartments were listed in US dollars in Q1 2026, rising to about 45% in Palermo. Since the 2023 repeal of the old rental law (DNU 70/2023), landlord and tenant can agree the currency and the price-adjustment index freely, so read the indexation clause before you sign.

Rent: The Biggest Line Item

Zonaprop's index tracks asking rents for a standard 50 m² dos ambientes (one-bedroom) apartment listed in pesos. In September 2026 the city average was ARS 898,154 a month (about $585), up 28.7% in a year in pesos.

Food and Dining

For eating out, Numbeo's October 2026 averages are about $18 for a meal at an inexpensive restaurant, $65 for a three-course dinner for two at a mid-range restaurant, $3.84 for a cappuccino and $5.58 for a mid-range bottle of wine at the shop. Supermarket shopping for one person cooking most meals runs about $200–$300 a month; for comparison, INDEC's basic food basket (the indigence-line food minimum, not an expat diet) was ARS 239,058 per adult in September 2026, about $156.

Transport

Public transport is still inexpensive. In October 2026 a subte (metro) ride costs ARS 1,817 (about $1.18) with a registered SUBE card, and the minimum city bus fare is about ARS 920 (about $0.60). The city updates fares monthly in line with inflation, and frequent subte riders get discounts after 20 trips. Someone who walks to most errands and rides the bus or subte a couple of times a day spends about $25–$40 a month.

Healthcare

Private health plans (prepagas) are the usual choice for expats who want private care. In July 2026 the main providers, such as OSDE, Swiss Medical and Galeno, mostly charged between about ARS 160,000 and ARS 840,000 a month (about $104–$547 at today's rate), depending on plan and age, and top plans exceeded ARS 1 million. Fees have kept rising since, by about 2% in October. A healthy adult on an entry-to-mid plan should budget roughly $110–$250 a month.

Utilities

A typical Greater Buenos Aires household paid about ARS 174,796 (about $114) for electricity, gas and water in July 2026, according to the UBA-CONICET IIEP institute, and Numbeo's average home internet plan is about $31. Budget about $100–$180 a month for a one-bedroom, more in winter.

Why Investors Are Watching Buenos Aires

Beyond lifestyle, Buenos Aires is attracting renewed investor attention. Dollar prices have partly recovered: Zonaprop's September 2026 index puts the city average at US$2,484/m², 11.3% below its peak. Gross rental yields average 5.7% citywide and 4.6% in Palermo. Frequently Asked Questions

How much do you need per month to live in Buenos Aires in 2026?

A single person renting a one-bedroom in a central neighbourhood should budget about $1,200–$1,900 a month. Numbeo puts costs before rent at about $881, and the average one-bedroom asking rent was about $585 in September 2026 (Zonaprop).

How much is rent for a one-bedroom in Palermo?

Zonaprop's September 2026 index puts the average asking rent for a 50 m² one-bedroom in Palermo at ARS 1,005,424 a month, about $655. Furnished and temporary rentals usually cost more.

Are Buenos Aires rents paid in dollars?

Mostly not. About 27.6% of Buenos Aires rental apartments were listed in US dollars in Q1 2026 (about 45% in Palermo), according to an IDECBA study of Argenprop listings. Since DNU 70/2023, the parties can agree any currency and adjustment index.
