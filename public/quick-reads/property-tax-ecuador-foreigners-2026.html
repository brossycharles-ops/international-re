Property Tax in Ecuador for Foreigners (2026 Rates)

By Charles Brossy October 30, 2026 3 min read

Property tax in Ecuador for foreigners works exactly as it does for citizens: the annual impuesto predial is a municipal tax on the property's cadastral value (land plus buildings), at a rate each council sets between 0.25 and 5 per thousand for urban property and 0.25 and 3 per thousand for rural land. On a home valued at US$100,000 in the cadastre, that band means roughly US$25 to US$500 a year before surcharges and discounts.

How the Ecuador Property Tax Is Calculated

The Organic Code of Territorial Organization (COOTAD) sets the rules. The taxable value is the land value plus any buildings (Art. 495), and councils must update cadastral values every two years (Art. 496). If you own several properties in the same canton, their values are added together before the rate is applied (Arts. 505 and 518). Vacant urban lots with basic services pay an extra 2 per thousand until they are built on, with some exceptions (Art. 507).

The tax is due during the calendar year and falls due on December 31 (Art. 512). Early payment earns a discount: 10% in the first half of January, falling every two weeks to 1% in late June. Payments from July 1 carry a 10% surcharge. Cuenca kept that schedule for urban property in 2026, and in December 2025 Quito's council approved an ordinance keeping 2025 rates, with no general increase and the same 10% January discount, though updated property valuations still change individual bills.

Exemption for Residents Aged 65 or More

Ecuador's Organic Law on Older Persons applies to foreigners living in Ecuador (Art. 2). Article 14 exempts anyone aged 65 or more from municipal taxes, including the predial, if their monthly income is at most 5 basic wages or their net worth is no more than 500 basic wages (US$2,410 and US$241,000 at the 2026 wage of US$482). Above those limits, tax is charged only on the excess. The law requires no prior declaration, but in practice retirees on Ecuador's pensioner visa should ask their municipality how it applies the exemption.

The annual tax is small next to the one-off alcabala paid on a purchase (a 1% base rate, plus any surcharges) (see our alcabala tip and the Ecuador buying guide).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do foreigners pay more property tax in Ecuador?

No. The predial is charged on the property's cadastral value at the rate the municipality sets, whoever the owner is: between 0.25 and 5 per thousand for urban property and 0.25 and 3 per thousand for rural land.

When is property tax due in Ecuador?

During the calendar year, by December 31. Paying in the first half of January earns a 10% discount, which shrinks every two weeks to 1% in the second half of June.

Are retirees exempt from property tax in Ecuador?

People aged 65 or more, including foreigners living in Ecuador, are exempt from municipal taxes if their monthly income is at most 5 basic wages or their net worth is at most 500 basic wages; above that, tax is due only on the excess.
