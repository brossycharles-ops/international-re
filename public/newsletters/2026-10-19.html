Peru's 3% Tax, Medellín Airbnb Rules, Panama Costs

Issue of October 19, 2026 · By Charles Brossy

Hi there,

This week we published new guides to Medellín's short-term rental rules, Peru's transfer tax and how long a Costa Rica purchase takes. Our fact-checkers also re-verified the Panama legal guide and the Buenos Aires cost-of-living figures against current sources.

This week's reading

Can foreigners buy property in Panama?
Our legal guide, rechecked on October 17: the 2% transfer tax is the seller's by law, buyers should budget roughly 2–3% for attorney, notary and registry, and the new Qualified Investor visa needs US$300,000 in new property or US$500,000 in resale.

Airbnb rules in Medellín for foreign owners
New this week: why any rental under 30 days must be registered in the Registro Nacional de Turismo (free, renewed between January 1 and March 31), why the building's bylaws must allow it, and the flat 35% tax non-residents pay on the rent.

Peru's alcabala: buyers pay 3%, first S/ 55,000 exempt
A one-minute read with the math: on a S/ 1,000,000 resale apartment the tax comes to S/ 28,350, about 2.8% of the price, and the contract cannot shift it to the seller.

Cost of living in Buenos Aires per month
Re-verified on October 18: a single person in Palermo or Belgrano should budget roughly $1,200–$1,900 a month including rent, with rent, food, transport, health plan and utility figures from current sources.

How long does it take to buy property in Costa Rica?
New this week: the five official transfer steps took 11 days in the World Bank's benchmark (May 2019 data), what that leaves out, and why beach concessions slow things down.

Most read: Best places to retire in Latin America
Our most-read article compares seven countries on what you can check: retiree visa income (US$1,000 a month for Panama's and Costa Rica's pension routes), cost-of-living data, property rules and US travel advisories.

Number of the week

8,322

In Medellín, “registered tourist homes rose from 521 in 2019 to 8,322 in 2026,” while the city reports 105 rentals suspended after 408 inspections. See the rules

What we corrected this week

Our Panama guide had overstated what buyers pay at closing: by law the 2% transfer tax is the seller's, and the beach strip that cannot be owned is 22 metres on the Pacific and 10 on the Caribbean. Our Buenos Aires budget had understated rent: Zonaprop puts the average Palermo one-bedroom at about $655 a month, and an IDECBA study of Argenprop listings found only 27.6% of rental apartments were listed in US dollars in Q1 2026.
