Our Story

About International RE

A global perspective on Latin American real estate — built through first-hand experience.

Why We Started This

International RE was founded by Charles Brossy on a simple idea: the best real estate insights come from people who've actually been there. After visiting 30+ countries across 4 continents, he saw a gap — clear, honest information about buying property in Latin America was almost impossible to find.

Most people hear about Costa Rica or Argentina and wonder: "Can I actually buy property there? Is it safe? What do prices look like?" We answer those questions with sourced data, legal context and an honest look at the risks.

What We Cover

Property, residency and cost of living for foreign buyers in 11 countries — Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama, Mexico, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Uruguay and Brazil — with in-depth guides to 17 cities, side-by-side market comparisons, and a free Monday newsletter.

How We Research

  • Every figure has a source. Prices, yields, taxes, fees and visa thresholds come from official sources first — tax and immigration agencies, central banks, statistics offices, official gazettes — then established data providers and major newspapers. Each article lists its sources at the end.
  • Independent fact-check. Before an article is published, a separate check re-opens every source and confirms the article says what the source says. Anything that can't be verified is removed, not guessed.
  • Continuous re-verification. Laws, prices and exchange rates change. Every article is re-checked against current sources on a rolling cycle, and its update date is refreshed when it changes.
  • Honest about risk. We flag official travel advisories, title problems, currency controls and thin resale markets — readers are making large financial decisions.
  • Independent. No commissions, no referral fees, no developer sponsorships.

International RE publishes general information, not legal, tax or investment advice. Always confirm details with a licensed local attorney before you buy.

Corrections

If you spot an error or an out-of-date figure, use our contact form with the page link. We check every report against current sources and correct the article, updating its date.
