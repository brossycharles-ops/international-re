Costa Rica

Pura Vida Living & Booming Property

Market Snapshot

🏠
Foreigners have full ownership rights

Unlike many countries, foreigners can own property outright with the same rights as citizens — no special permits or local partners needed.

📈
San José new/prime apartments: about US$2,902/m²

Global Property Guide's July 2026 survey; Costa Rica's national average gross yield was 7.63% in Q2 2026. Coastal Guanacaste is carrying unsold high-end inventory while the Central Valley gains buyers. October 2026 market update.

🌴
Municipal property tax of 0.25%

The annual municipal tax is 0.25% of the registered value. Homes whose construction value exceeds ₡143 million (2026; adjusted each December) also pay the Luxury Home Tax (Impuesto Solidario) of 0.25–0.55% on a progressive scale, charged on the whole value including land. Costa Rica buying guide.

✈️
A strong colón raises local costs

The colón traded around 450 per dollar in August 2026, against about 685 in mid-2022, so construction, closing fees and upkeep cost more in dollar terms even where asking prices are flat.

💰
Guanacaste beach condos: about $2,000–$3,000/m²

Asking prices from a small El Financiero sample (August 2026); there is no reliable published rental-yield figure for the coast, so ask for real booking records. Guanacaste guide, sourced.

Nicaragua

Low Prices, High Political Risk

Market Snapshot

💵
Legal to buy, but high-risk

The US State Department's 2026 Investment Climate Statement reports no specific restrictions on foreign buyers outside border and national-security areas, but also reports that the government has seized foreign-owned property. Granada buying risks, sourced.

🏠
US retirees' homes taken in 2026

In August 2026 the government took nine homes owned by US retirees in Tola, Rivas, on the Pacific coast (La Prensa); eight were returned in October. The US rates Nicaragua Level 3, "Reconsider travel".

📜
A new constitution in 2025

The 2025 reform allows expropriation "with fair compensation" and dropped officials' personal liability for confiscations. A 2025 law also declared a 15 km strip along land borders state property, and the property registry now sits under the Attorney General's office.

📍
Little reliable price data

There is no official house-price index. Numbeo's crowd-sourced Managua data puts city-centre apartments at about $1,190/m² (October 2026). Treat agency price and yield claims with suspicion.

🌎
Tourism is below its 2017 peak

International arrivals were 1.08 million in 2025, against 1.86 million in 2017 (Banco Central de Nicaragua), which weakens the case for short-term rental income.

Argentina

World-Class Living at Emerging Prices

Market Snapshot

🏙️
Buenos Aires: US$2,484/m² city-wide average

Average asking price in Zonaprop's September 2026 index, 11.3% below its all-time high. Palermo, the main expat neighborhood, averages US$3,454/m². Argentina guide.

💵
Priced and mostly paid in US dollars

Properties are listed and sold in US dollars and most buyers pay cash. Mortgages are returning but still small: Zonaprop reports 17% of Buenos Aires City deeds used one in August 2026, mostly by residents with local income.

📈
Gross rental yields of about 4.5–6% in USD

Zonaprop puts the Buenos Aires City average at 5.7% gross (Palermo 4.6%). Net yields are lower once building fees, taxes, vacancy and management are paid.

🍷
Mendoza wine country

Boutique vineyard properties with Andes views. There is no reliable price index for Mendoza, so get local comparables and legal advice before you buy.

📋
No restrictions on foreign buyers

Foreigners can purchase property with an Argentine tax ID (a CUIT since March 2026). The process is straightforward with a local notary (escribano) handling the transaction.

🇨🇱

Chile

South America's Safest Bet

Market Snapshot

🏆
Foreigners can buy in their own name

Chile's Civil Code gives foreigners the same civil rights as Chileans. You need a RUT tax ID first, and some state land near borders and the coast is restricted.

🏙️
Santiago: about US$4,300–4,900/m² in premium areas

New apartments in Providencia, Las Condes and Vitacura averaged 103–117 UF/m² in TOCTOC data to July 2026; the metro average was 83 UF (about US$3,500). Santiago vs the Lake District · Santiago neighborhood guide.

📈
Lower volatility than most of the region

Chile's property market has been less volatile than many Latin American markets, though Santiago has spent the past two years absorbing excess inventory.

📚
Mortgages mainly for residents

Chilean banks lend mostly to residents with local income. Non-residents face stricter checks and larger down payments, so most foreign purchases are paid in cash.

🏔️
Lake District: a lifestyle market

Pucón, Villarrica and Puerto Varas offer lake houses among volcanoes and forests, but reliable price data is thin. Compare it with Santiago.
