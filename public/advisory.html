FAQ

Common questions

Are you a licensed real estate advisor? +

No. International RE provides educational research and introduction services. We are not licensed in any jurisdiction. All transactions require local licensed counsel.

Do you take referral fees from brokers? +

No. Our fee is the only fee. This ensures recommendations are unbiased.

What countries do you cover? +

Primary coverage: Costa Rica, Panama, Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Nicaragua, Uruguay, Dominican Republic, and Ecuador. We are expanding to Peru, Brazil, and select European and Asian markets.

What if I want a country you don't cover? +

We'll be upfront about our coverage limits and can recommend trusted contacts in markets we don't cover directly.

What's the refund policy? +

Tier 1 strategy calls are non-refundable. Tier 2 and 3 engagements have a satisfaction clause — if the deliverable doesn't meet the agreed scope, we'll revise or issue a partial refund.

How is this different from the free newsletter? +

The newsletter covers markets broadly. Advisory is personalized to YOUR budget, timeline, risk tolerance, and target markets. You also get direct broker and attorney introductions.

Can you help with financing? +

We can introduce you to banks that lend to foreigners in certain markets (notably Chile and Panama). We do not provide financial advice or arrange financing.

I'm not ready for advisory. What should I do? +

Subscribe to the free newsletter and take the market quiz. When you're ready, we'll be here.
